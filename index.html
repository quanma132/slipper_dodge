Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Slipper Dodge handles information when you play our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not require users to register an account with real-name personal details. However, to provide core features, we and our third-party services collect certain data:

  • Leaderboard Display Name: If you submit a score to the global Leaderboard, we collect and publicly display the custom nickname or username you choose to enter, along with your high score. Please do not use your real name or sensitive information as your display name.
  • In-App Purchase (IAP) Data: Purchases made in Slipper Dodge are processed entirely through the Google Play Billing system. We do not store or access your credit card details or financial credentials. We only receive purchase confirmations to deliver your digital items.
  • Device & Usage Data: Automatically collected non-personal data including Advertising ID, device model, operating system version, gameplay stats, and crash reports.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Operate the game, save your progress, and process in-app purchases.
  • Display your nickname and score on the public Leaderboard.
  • Serve personalized and non-personalized advertisements.
  • Analyze game performance, fix bugs, and optimize player experience.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Our game uses trusted third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, and platform services. These third parties may collect device identifiers and usage data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Games: For core integration and Leaderboard functionality.
  • Google AdMob: For displaying banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads.
  • Google Play Billing: For secure handling of In-App Purchases.

You can review Google’s Privacy Policy here: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Children’s Privacy

Slipper Dodge is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in applicable jurisdictions). If you believe a child has submitted personal data without parental consent, please contact us to have it deleted.

5. Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Your Leaderboard nickname and scores are stored securely and retained for as long as the Leaderboard service is active.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be updated directly on this page with a new Effective Date.
